The Seter Artist
Artist Worshiper Creator Faith Storyteller
"Every good and perfect gift comes from above." — James 1:17

I am an artist, a worshiper, and a creator who moves through the world with intention, tenderness, and a heart that belongs to God. I love cartoons, animation, illustrations, colour, and the quiet joy of sitting in His presence.

Everything beautiful in me begins with Him. Art taught me wonder. Worship taught me surrender. Every brushstroke, every prayer, every illustration is an offering back to the One who imagined me first.

The Seter is my secret place — and I want it to be yours too. A space where faith meets creativity, where young people can ask hard questions about God, and where the beauty of His Word is made visible through art.
